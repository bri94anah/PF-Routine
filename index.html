💡 Quick Tips


  • Free Rack Squats: Set your stance comfortably, keep your chest up, and maintain controlled movement throughout.
  • Rest Periods: Rest 60 to 90 seconds between each set.
  • Weight Selection: Choose a weight where the last 2-3 reps feel challenging.
